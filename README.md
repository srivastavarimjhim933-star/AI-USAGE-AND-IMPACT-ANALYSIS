# 🤖 AI Usage & Impact Analysis 📊

### 🚀 Exploring How Artificial Intelligence Impacts Students & Professionals

Welcome to **AI Usage & Impact Analysis**, a Business Analytics project developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to explore the usage, popularity, and impact of Artificial Intelligence tools in everyday life.

🌌 This project presents a modern, futuristic approach to data visualization, transforming raw data into meaningful insights through an interactive business analytics dashboard.

The analysis focuses on how students and professionals use AI tools, which tools they prefer, what tasks they perform with AI, and how they perceive its impact on productivity, time savings, and satisfaction.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

✨ The main objectives of this project are:

🔹 Analyze AI usage patterns among students and professionals.

🔹 Identify the most popular AI tools based on the available dataset.

🔹 Understand the major purposes of AI usage, including coding, studying, writing, research, and data analysis.

🔹 Evaluate reported productivity scores and time savings.

🔹 Compare productivity and satisfaction scores between user groups.

🔹 Analyze users' willingness to recommend AI tools.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 🤖 1. AI Tool Popularity

ChatGPT leads the displayed AI tool categories with approximately 133 users, followed by Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, and Claude.

This highlights the popularity of ChatGPT within the dataset and provides an opportunity to explore differences in AI tool adoption.

### 🎓 2. Students vs Professionals

The dataset includes 158 students (52.67%) and 142 professionals (47.33%).

Both groups are well represented, allowing us to explore how AI tools are used in academic and professional environments.

### 💡 3. Major AI Use Cases

The dashboard explores eight major use cases:

💻 Coding  
🎨 Content Creation  
🎧 Customer Support  
📊 Data Analysis  
🗂️ Office Work  
🔎 Research  
📚 Studying  
✍️ Writing

These categories illustrate the different ways AI tools can support learning, creativity, communication, and workplace activities.

### ⚡ 4. Productivity & Satisfaction

The dashboard compares total productivity and satisfaction scores across students and professionals.

Students have a total productivity score of 860 and a satisfaction score of 975, while professionals have totals of 770 and 845, respectively.

These are total scores rather than averages, so the different group sizes should be considered when interpreting the results.

### 💬 5. User Recommendation Analysis

The recommendation results show:

✅ **Yes:** 183 users — 61%

❌ **No:** 73 users — 24.33%

🤔 **Maybe:** 44 users — 14.67%

The majority of users would recommend AI tools, suggesting generally positive sentiment within the dataset.
